To get Weflix on your Android phone, tablet or TV, follow the steps below.

Install Weflix on Android

Weflix isn't on Google Play. It's shared privately with friends and family as a direct download.

Download Weflix

Getting started

You'll need an Android phone, tablet or Android TV, and a Weflix account. If you don't have one yet, sign up at weflix.me first.

Installing on a phone or tablet

  1. Download Weflix using the button above.
  2. Allow the install. Android may warn that the file comes from outside Google Play. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Open the downloaded file, tap Install, then Open.
  4. Sign in with the email and password you signed up with at weflix.me.

Installing on Android TV

  1. Install the Downloader app from Google Play on your TV.
  2. Open Downloader and type Weflix.me/downloads/TV
  3. Choose Install. If the TV blocks it, allow Downloader to install apps when asked.
  4. Open Weflix and sign in with the email and password you signed up with at weflix.me.

Updates

When there's a new version, download it again from this page and install it over the old one. Your login and settings stay.
