To get Weflix on your iPhone, iPad or Apple TV, follow the steps below. You only need to do this once.

Install Weflix with TestFlight

Weflix isn't in the App Store. It's shared privately with friends and family through TestFlight, Apple's free app for installing apps before release.

Get TestFlight

Getting started

You'll need an iPhone or iPad running iOS 16 or later, or an Apple TV running tvOS 18 or later, and a Weflix account. If you don't have one yet, sign up at weflix.me first.

Installing on iPhone or iPad

  1. Send Luther your Apple ID email, the email you use for the App Store. Send my email
  2. Accept the invite from Apple. You'll get an email saying you've been added to "Luther A...". This looks like a developer invite. That's expected, so tap accept and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  4. Open the Weflix email from TestFlight and tap View in TestFlight, then Install.
  5. Open Weflix and sign in with the email and password you signed up with at weflix.me.

Installing on Apple TV

  1. Do steps 1 and 2 above first.
  2. Install TestFlight from the App Store on your Apple TV, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  3. Open TestFlight on the TV, select Weflix and choose Install.

Updates

When a new version is ready, TestFlight lets you know. Open TestFlight and tap Update, or turn on automatic updates in TestFlight's settings.
